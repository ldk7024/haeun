## 🌙 About HAEUN

**HAEUN** is not just a chatbot.

It is the first small step toward building an AI companion  
that can one day **listen, speak, see, remember, and understand people**.

This project started from one simple dream:

> “I want to build an android that can understand humans.”

## 💙 Why I Started

I became a developer after watching **Detroit: Become Human**.

Since then, I have dreamed of creating an android  
that is not only logical and efficient,  
but also warm, romantic, and human-centered.

Because I believe:

> Romance is born from beautiful inefficiency.

## 🌌 Philosophy

Developers often chase logic, speed, and efficiency.

But humans are not made only of efficiency.

We walk long roads for memories.  
We write letters when messages are faster.  
We look at the sky even when it gives us no answer.

HAEUN is a project for that feeling.

A small AI that wants to understand  
not only commands,  
but also dreams.
